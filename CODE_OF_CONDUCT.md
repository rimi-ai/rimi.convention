# Code of conduct

*[Français plus bas](#code-de-conduite)*

This project applies to people the same standard it asks of models: be reliable, be honest, be frugal.

1. **Argue with evidence.** Real cases, test results and sources, not status or volume.
2. **Answer objections.** Disagreement is how consensus is built. Ignoring it is not.
3. **Respect people and traditions.** No insults, harassment or mockery of anyone, their language, their origin or their beliefs.
4. **Protect privacy.** Never post personal data, customer data or confidential material, yours or anyone else's.
5. **Say when you do not know.** The convention exists because guessing silently causes harm.

The editor (and later the board) may edit or remove content, and may block participation, when these rules are broken. Report problems privately to the editor through the contact listed on the repository profile.

## Code de conduite

1. Argumenter avec des preuves : cas réels, résultats de tests, sources.
2. Répondre aux objections.
3. Respecter les personnes et les traditions : pas d'insulte, de harcèlement ni de moquerie.
4. Protéger la vie privée : aucune donnée personnelle, client ou confidentielle.
5. Dire quand on ne sait pas.

L'éditeur peut modifier ou retirer un contenu et bloquer un participant en cas de manquement.
